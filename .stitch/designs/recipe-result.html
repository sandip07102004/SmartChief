Creamy Tuscan Garlic Chicken

A decadent, restaurant-quality dish featuring tender pan-seared chicken breasts smothered in a rich garlic cream sauce with sun-dried tomatoes and fresh spinach.

timer 25 min
local_fire_department 420 kcal
group 4 serv
equalizer Medium

Ingredients

Instructions

1

Sear the chicken until golden brown.

Season chicken with salt, pepper, and Italian seasoning. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Cook chicken for 5-7 minutes per side until cooked through. Remove and set aside.

2

Sauté garlic and sun-dried tomatoes in the same pan.

In the same skillet, add the minced garlic and sun-dried tomatoes. Sauté for 1-2 minutes until fragrant, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom of the pan.

3

Add cream and parmesan, simmer to reduce.

Reduce heat to medium-low. Pour in the heavy cream and bring to a gentle simmer. Stir in the grated parmesan cheese until the sauce is smooth and begins to thicken.

4

Stir in spinach until wilted and return chicken to pan.

Add the baby spinach and let it simmer in the sauce until wilted. Return the cooked chicken breasts to the skillet, spooning the sauce over them. Serve immediately.
