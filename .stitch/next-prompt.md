A dedicated full-screen hands-free cooking mode for "CookSmart" with large type, countdown cooking timers, step progression, and voice prompt indicators.

**DESIGN SYSTEM (REQUIRED):**
- Theme: Dark mobile culinary app with deep charcoal (#101318) and elevated card surface (#191D24).
- Accent: Warm saffron orange (#FF6B35) for primary actions, active indicators, and badges.
- Text: Crisp snow white (#F9FAFB) for headings and high contrast titles; muted slate (#94A3B8) for secondary details.
- Styling: Rounded cards (radius 20px), pill chips (radius 9999px), 1px borders (rgba(255,255,255,0.08)).
- Typography: Clean modern sans-serif (Outfit / Plus Jakarta Sans), track-tight headlines.
- Safe Area: Mobile interface with top status bar styling and bottom navigation dock (Home, Ingredients/Pantry, Saved).

**Page Structure:**
1. Top bar with exit cooking mode (✕), recipe title, and total progress indicator (Step 2 of 4).
2. Large prominent countdown cooking timer with start/pause buttons and sound alerts.
3. High-legibility large step card with crystal-clear instructions readable from across the kitchen counter.
4. Voice command mic button with listening state ("Next step", "Repeat step", "Set 5 min timer").
5. Bottom navigation controls: Large "Previous Step" and "Next Step" buttons.
